#  Task 3: Data Visualization & Dashboarding

## Project Overview

This project focuses on building an interactive business intelligence dashboard using the **Sample Superstore Dataset**. The objective was to analyze sales performance, profit trends, product performance, and regional insights through effective data visualization techniques using **Power BI Desktop** and **Python**.

---

## Objectives

* Build an executive dashboard with key business metrics.
* Analyze sales and profit across different product categories.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Visualize state-wise sales and profit distribution.
* Highlight the top-performing products.
* Create interactive reports using slicers and filters.

---

## Dashboard Features

*  KPI Cards

  * Total Sales
  * Total Profit
  * Total Orders

*  Sales by Category (Clustered Bar Chart)

*  Profit by Category (Clustered Bar Chart)

*  Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)

*  Top 10 Products by Sales

*  Sales by State (Map)

*  Profit by State (Map)

*  Interactive Slicers

  * Region
  * Category

## Key Insights

* Office Supplies generated the highest sales among all categories.
* Technology contributed significantly to overall profit.
* Sales increased steadily toward the end of the year.
* Top-performing products accounted for a major share of total sales.
* Regional analysis identified variations in sales and profitability across different states.
